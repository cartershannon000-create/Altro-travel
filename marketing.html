Why Altro

A travel app for what you actually loved.

Most travel apps track plans. Altro tracks taste — what stayed with you, what you'd return to first, and what belongs on your personal map.

Rank

Every city, scored 1 to 10.

Log a city, sort it into a sentiment bucket, then sharpen the order with quick head-to-head comparisons. Your scores recalculate live as your list grows.

Discover

Find cities that match your taste.

Swipe through places tuned to what you actually love. Save the ones that fit, skip the ones that don't — Altro learns your taste with every swipe.

Weekly Picks

Curated cities, tuned to you.

Every week, hand-picked destinations matched to the places you've already ranked. Similar to what you love, trending among people like you, and right for the season.

World Map

Your travel life, on a single map.

Every ranked and saved city becomes part of a living map of your taste. Filter by visited, saved, or all — and see your footprint at a glance.

City Match

Know if a city fits before you go.

Every city is scored against your taste across six attributes — cuisine, walkability, livability, nightlife, affordability, and safety — so you know which places will feel like home.

Social

See what your friends are ranking.

Follow people whose taste you trust. Discover cities through their real rankings, compare your travel profiles, and find places you both would love.

How It Works

Light to use. Honest about your taste.

No spreadsheets, no overthinking. Log a city, react to it, sharpen with comparisons, and watch your map grow.

Add a city

Start with where you've been and a quick sentiment — loved it, liked it, or it was fine.

Compare two

Pick the one you'd return to first. A few rounds is enough to sharpen the order.

Rate attributes

Score cuisine, walkability, nightlife, and more — Altro learns what you actually value.

Discover what's next

Get curated picks and taste-matched cities the moment your list is alive.

A quiet record of the places that pulled you forward — and a compass for the next ones that will.
