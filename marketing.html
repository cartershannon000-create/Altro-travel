Inside Altro

Built for the way you actually travel.

Weekly Picks

Curated cities, tuned to you.

Every week, handpicked destinations matched to the places you've already ranked. Similar to what you love, trending among people like you, and right for the season.

  • Picks every Sunday morning.
  • Matched against your scores, not generic trends.

Saved

A wishlist for the cities you'll come back to.

Save any city the moment it catches you. Browse your wishlist in one place, sort by score or recency, and decide where to go next.

  • Save cities with one tap.

The Method

Simple to use, tailored to your travel taste.

No spreadsheets, no overthinking. Log a city, rate what you loved, sharpen with comparisons, and watch your map grow.

1

Add a city

Start with where you've been and a quick sentiment. Loved it, liked it, or it was fine.

2

Rate Attributes

Score cuisine, walkability, nightlife, and more. Altro learns what you actually value.

3

Compare against your list

Pick the one you'd return to first. A few rounds is enough to sharpen the order.

4

Discover what's next

Get curated picks and taste matched cities the moment your list is alive.

A quiet record of the places that pulled you forward, and a compass for the next ones that will.
